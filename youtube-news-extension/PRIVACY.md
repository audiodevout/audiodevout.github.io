# Privacy Policy: News Button for YouTube

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

News Button for YouTube ("the extension") is a Chrome extension. It adds a
News section back to YouTube's sidebar and opens YouTube's News page for the
countries you choose. This policy says what the extension does and doesn't do
with your information.

## Summary

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
The developer has no server and receives nothing from you.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves one thing: **which countries you've switched on** (for
example `US, IN, NL`).

- It is saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`.
- If Chrome Sync is turned on, Google syncs this list between your own
  signed-in Chrome browsers, the same way it syncs your other extension
  settings. That syncing is governed by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by
  the developer.
- The developer cannot see this list.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect your name, email, browsing history, watch history,
  location, or any other personal information.
- On YouTube pages it only adds a News section to the left sidebar. To put
  the section in the right place it looks at which links are in the sidebar.
  Nothing else on the page is read, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- It does not make network requests of its own. Its font is included in the
  extension. When you click a News link, your browser opens a normal
  `youtube.com` page, just as if you had typed the address yourself.
- It does not sell or transfer data to third parties, and does not use data
  for creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to its single
  purpose.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Remembers which countries you've switched on. |
| Access to `https://www.youtube.com/*` | Adds the News section to YouTube's sidebar. |

## YouTube

When you open a YouTube page, YouTube and Google handle your visit under
their own terms and privacy policy. The extension is not made by, endorsed
by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.
