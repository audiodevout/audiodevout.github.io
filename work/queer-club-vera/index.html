Queerclub VERA

VJ

2 October 2026 · VERA · Groningen, NL

I played live visuals for the fourth edition of Queerclub VERA at VERA, Groningen. The night, billed MASCMAXXING PARTY, ran Downstage from 22:00 to 03:00. RileyPeeP opened, Coco Coquelicot followed with disco, deep house, and Detroit techno, and Bloemenvaes closed.
