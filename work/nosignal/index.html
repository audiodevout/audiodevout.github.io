NoSignal

INSTALLATION

NoSignal is an installation made in collaboration with 3D artist and environment designer Beatrice Fini, for Urban Arts Tour: Work in Progress, part of Let's Gro Festival 2026 at Forum Groningen. Five old monitors lie piled and propped among bricks and metal rods, the leftovers of building. Some are switched on with nothing plugged in, showing only their own standby messages: No Signal, Check signal cable, Searching for input. Others are dark, or turned around to show the bare casing behind the screen. The work looks at how we treat technology, and at the extraction and forgetting of the resources it is built from. Any piece of tech, a monitor or a keyboard, is made of elements pulled from the earth in many different places and brought together to work for us for a while. For the consumer, the end of a product's life is the end of their interaction with it. Its existence carries on. Materials taken from all over the world stay bound together inside a plastic shell that was used for a few years and then forgotten. In a thousand, a hundred thousand or a million years, those elements may still sit together, only because we decided to use them for a decade. Shown at Forum Groningen on 7 November 2026.
