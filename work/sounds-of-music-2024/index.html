Hackathon with Huba de Graaff

Hackathon

November 2024 · Frank Mohr Institute · Groningen, NL

I took part in a hackathon led by composer Huba de Graaff during Soundsofmusic 2024. With MADtech at the Frank Mohr Institute in Groningen, we worked with reel-to-reel recorders, tape loops, and other old sound carriers, then presented the setup in the festival exhibition.
